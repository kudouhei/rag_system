# Amendment — Mandatory Liquidity Management Tools for Money Market Funds (REG-FM-105A)

## Article 1 — Background

Following the MFCA's 2024 thematic review of money market fund resilience
during periods of market stress (see CIRC-2024-03), this Amendment
introduces mandatory liquidity management tools for money market funds
(MMFs), effective 1 July 2025. It amends Article 4 of REG-FM-105.

## Article 2 — Mandatory Swing Pricing for MMFs

From the Effective Date, all Meridia-domiciled money market funds must
implement swing pricing or an equivalent anti-dilution levy mechanism.
Voluntary adoption, previously permitted under REG-FM-105 Article 4, is no
longer sufficient for MMFs; the tool must be embedded in the Fund's
constitutional documents and disclosed in the Prospectus.

## Article 3 — Minimum Daily and Weekly Liquid Asset Thresholds

A money market fund must hold, at all times:

1. At least 10% of net assets in daily liquid assets (cash, overnight
   deposits, or instruments maturing within 1 business day);
2. At least 30% of net assets in weekly liquid assets (instruments
   maturing within 5 business days, or the daily liquid assets referenced
   in item 1).

Breach of either threshold must be reported to the MFCA within 1 business
day, together with a remediation plan.

## Article 4 — Enhanced Stress Testing Frequency

For money market funds, the quarterly stress testing requirement in
Article 3 of REG-FM-105 is increased to monthly, with results reported to
the MFCA within 10 business days of each test (previously 15 business
days under REG-FM-105).

## Article 5 — Transitional Provisions

Money market funds in operation before the Effective Date have a 6-month
transitional period, ending 31 December 2025, to bring swing pricing
mechanisms and constitutional documents into compliance. New money market
funds launched on or after 1 July 2025 must comply immediately.

## Article 6 — Status of Superseded Provisions

To the extent Article 4 of REG-FM-105 permitted money market funds to rely
solely on redemption gates without an anti-dilution mechanism, that
provision is superseded by this Amendment for money market funds only.
REG-FM-105 continues to apply in full to all other open-ended fund types.
