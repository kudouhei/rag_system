# Net Asset Value (NAV) Calculation and Valuation Policy (REG-FM-102)

## Article 1 — Valuation Frequency

A Fund Manager must calculate the Net Asset Value (NAV) per unit at a
frequency consistent with the Fund's dealing frequency disclosed in the
Prospectus, and in any case no less frequently than:

1. Daily, for open-ended Funds offering daily dealing;
2. Weekly, for Funds with weekly or lower dealing frequency;
3. At each dealing point, for Funds admitting subscriptions/redemptions on
   a bespoke schedule.

## Article 2 — Valuation Methodology

Portfolio assets must be valued using the following hierarchy:

1. **Level 1** — Quoted prices in active markets, used without adjustment;
2. **Level 2** — Observable inputs other than quoted prices (e.g. matrix
   pricing, broker quotes corroborated by at least two independent
   sources);
3. **Level 3** — Unobservable inputs, requiring a documented valuation
   model approved by the Fund's Valuation Committee and reviewed by the
   Depositary.

A Fund Manager must disclose, in its annual report, the proportion of net
assets valued at each level of the hierarchy.

## Article 3 — NAV Error Correction

If a NAV calculation error exceeds the materiality threshold below, the
Fund Manager must correct the NAV, notify the Depositary within 1 business
day, and compensate affected investors:

| Fund Type              | Materiality Threshold |
|-------------------------|------------------------|
| Money market fund       | 0.10% of NAV per unit  |
| Standard open-ended fund| 0.50% of NAV per unit  |
| Complex/alternative fund| 1.00% of NAV per unit  |

Compensation must be paid within 15 business days of the error being
confirmed. Errors below the threshold must still be logged in the Fund's
error register and reported to the MFCA in the quarterly compliance return.

## Article 4 — Independent Pricing Verification

At least one pricing source used for Level 1 and Level 2 assets must be
independent of the Fund Manager's trading desk. The Depositary is
responsible for performing independent NAV oversight checks and must
escalate any discrepancy greater than 0.25% to the Fund Manager's
compliance function within 1 business day.

## Article 5 — Suspension of NAV Publication

A Fund Manager may suspend publication of the NAV only where:

1. A material portion (>10% of net assets) of the portfolio cannot be
   reliably priced due to market disruption;
2. The primary market for the Fund's underlying assets is closed or
   suspended; or
3. Suspension is otherwise necessary to protect the interests of investors,
   subject to Article 3 of REG-FM-106 (Suspensions).

Any suspension must be reported to the MFCA within 24 hours and disclosed
to investors without delay.

## Article 6 — Late Disclosure Penalties

Failure to publish the NAV within the timeframe disclosed in the Prospectus,
without an approved suspension under Article 5, constitutes a reportable
incident. Repeated late publication (three or more instances within a
rolling 12-month period) will be referred for enforcement review.
