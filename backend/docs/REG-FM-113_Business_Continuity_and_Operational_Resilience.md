# Business Continuity and Operational Resilience (REG-FM-113)

## Article 1 — Important Business Services

A Fund Manager must identify its "Important Business Services" — those
whose disruption could cause harm to investors or market integrity —
including NAV calculation, dealing/settlement, custody instructions, and
investor communications.

## Article 2 — Impact Tolerances

For each Important Business Service, the Fund Manager must set a maximum
tolerable disruption period ("impact tolerance") and demonstrate, through
testing, that it can remain within that tolerance under severe but
plausible scenarios, including:

1. Loss of a primary data centre or cloud region;
2. Unavailability of a critical outsourced service provider (see
   REG-FM-112);
3. A cyber-attack resulting in loss of system availability or data
   integrity.

## Article 3 — Testing and Review

Business continuity arrangements must be tested at least annually, and
the results, including any impact tolerance breaches identified during
testing, must be reported to the governing body and retained for 5 years.

## Article 4 — Incident Notification

A Fund Manager must notify the MFCA within 24 hours of any operational
incident that:

1. Causes an Important Business Service to breach its impact tolerance;
2. Results in a NAV publication delay (see Article 6 of REG-FM-102);
3. Compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of investor
   data.

The notification must include an initial assessment of investor impact,
root cause (if known), and remediation actions taken.

## Article 5 — Communication with Investors

Where an operational incident materially affects investors' ability to
subscribe, redeem, or obtain accurate information about their holdings,
the Fund Manager must communicate with affected investors within 48 hours,
including expected resolution timelines and any interim measures (e.g.
extended dealing deadlines).

## Article 6 — Third-Country Data Processing

Where investor data is processed outside the Republic of Meridia, the
Fund Manager must ensure equivalent data protection safeguards apply and
must be able to demonstrate this to the MFCA upon request, consistent with
the Fund Manager's obligations under Article 6 of REG-FM-112.

## Article 7 — Annual Resilience Self-Assessment

Fund Managers must submit an annual Operational Resilience Self-Assessment
to the MFCA, summarising impact tolerances, testing outcomes, and material
changes to the technology or outsourcing footprint since the prior
assessment.
