# Guidance Circular — Stress Testing for Money Market Funds (CIRC-2024-03)

## 1. Purpose

This Circular provides non-binding guidance on the stress testing
methodology expected by the MFCA for money market funds (MMFs) under
Article 3 of REG-FM-105. It follows the MFCA's 2024 thematic review of
MMF resilience during periods of market-wide liquidity stress observed in
comparable jurisdictions.

## 2. Recommended Stress Scenarios

The MFCA expects MMF stress testing programmes to include, at minimum:

1. **Redemption shock scenario** — simulate simultaneous redemption
   requests of 20%, 40%, and 60% of net assets within a single dealing
   day, and assess the Fund's ability to meet these requests without
   breaching the liquid asset thresholds in REG-FM-105A;
2. **Credit spread widening scenario** — simulate a 100–300 basis point
   widening of credit spreads on the Fund's commercial paper and corporate
   debt holdings, and the resulting mark-to-market impact on NAV;
3. **Sponsor support unavailability scenario** — assume no implicit or
   explicit sponsor support is available, consistent with the
   prohibition on sponsor support reliance under REG-FM-105A.

## 3. Reverse Stress Testing

In addition to the forward-looking scenarios above, the MFCA recommends
Fund Managers perform reverse stress testing — identifying the
combination of redemption volume and market conditions that would cause
the Fund to breach its weekly liquid asset threshold — to better
understand the Fund's specific vulnerabilities.

## 4. Governance Expectations

Stress test results, including any near-breaches of the thresholds in
REG-FM-105A, should be presented to the Fund's governing body (see
REG-FM-110) with a clear narrative on emerging risks, not only quantitative
output. The MFCA does not prescribe a specific stress testing software
or model but expects the methodology to be documented and independently
reviewed at least annually.

## 5. Interaction with Mandatory Requirements

This Circular does not itself impose binding obligations; the binding
monthly stress testing frequency and reporting deadlines for money market
funds are set out in Article 4 of REG-FM-105A. Where this Circular's
recommended scenarios conflict with a future binding rule, the binding
rule prevails.

## 6. Review

The MFCA will review this Circular within 18 months of publication in
light of industry feedback and observed market conditions.
