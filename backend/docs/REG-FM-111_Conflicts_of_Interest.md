# Conflicts of Interest and Related-Party Transactions (REG-FM-111)

## Article 1 — Conflicts of Interest Policy

A Fund Manager must maintain a written Conflicts of Interest Policy
identifying circumstances that give rise, or could give rise, to a
conflict between the interests of the Fund Manager (or its staff) and the
interests of the Fund or its investors, including:

1. Allocation of investment opportunities across multiple Funds managed by
   the same Fund Manager;
2. Personal account dealing by employees with access to non-public
   information about Fund transactions;
3. Receipt of inducements, soft commissions, or research payments from
   brokers or counterparties;
4. Transactions between the Fund and an affiliate of the Fund Manager.

## Article 2 — Related-Party Transactions

A transaction between a Fund and a related party (the Fund Manager, its
affiliates, or a member of the governing body) is permitted only where:

1. It is executed on arm's-length terms, evidenced by at least two
   independent quotations or a documented fair-value assessment;
2. It is pre-approved by a majority of independent members of the
   governing body under Article 5 of REG-FM-110;
3. It is disclosed in the Fund's annual report, including the counterparty,
   nature, and value of the transaction.

## Article 3 — Allocation of Investment Opportunities

Where an investment opportunity is suitable for more than one Fund managed
by the same Fund Manager, allocation must follow a documented, pre-defined
methodology (e.g. pro-rata by net asset value) applied consistently, and
records of each allocation decision must be retained for 5 years.

## Article 4 — Personal Account Dealing

Employees of the Fund Manager with access to material non-public
information about Fund transactions must pre-clear personal securities
transactions with the compliance function and are subject to a minimum
30-calendar-day holding period on any security also held by a Fund they
support.

## Article 5 — Inducements and Research Payments

Research and execution costs must be unbundled: research payments may not
be bundled with trading commissions unless separately disclosed and
budgeted, and any inducement received in connection with the distribution
of Fund units must be disclosed to investors and must not impair the Fund
Manager's duty to act in investors' best interests.

## Article 6 — Escalation and Reporting

Material conflicts that cannot be adequately managed through disclosure or
mitigation must be escalated to the governing body and, where they affect
investor outcomes, reported to the MFCA within 10 business days of
identification.

## Article 7 — Sanctions

Failure to maintain an adequate Conflicts of Interest Policy, or a
material undisclosed related-party transaction, is treated as a serious
compliance failure and may result in disgorgement of any profit derived
from the conflicted transaction, in addition to administrative fines.
