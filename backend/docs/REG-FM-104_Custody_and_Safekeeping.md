# Custody and Safekeeping of Fund Assets (REG-FM-104)

## Article 1 — Mandatory Independent Depositary

Every Fund must appoint a single, independent Depositary that is separate
from, and not controlled by, the Fund Manager. The Depositary must be
authorised by the MFCA to provide custody and depositary services and must
maintain professional indemnity insurance of at least MER 5,000,000.

## Article 2 — Segregation of Assets

The Depositary must hold Fund assets in segregated accounts, clearly
identified as belonging to the Fund, separate from:

1. The Depositary's own proprietary assets;
2. Assets of other clients of the Depositary;
3. Assets of the Fund Manager or its affiliates.

Cash held on behalf of the Fund must be placed with eligible credit
institutions and reconciled daily against the Fund's accounting records.

## Article 3 — Depositary Oversight Duties

The Depositary must independently verify:

1. That the Fund Manager's instructions comply with the Prospectus and
   applicable law before execution;
2. That NAV calculations under REG-FM-102 are performed correctly;
3. That income due to the Fund (dividends, coupon payments, corporate
   action proceeds) is collected in a timely manner;
4. That subscription and redemption proceeds are settled within the
   timeframe disclosed in the Prospectus.

## Article 4 — Sub-Custody Arrangements

Where the Depositary delegates safekeeping to a sub-custodian (e.g. for
assets held in a foreign market), it must:

1. Conduct due diligence on the sub-custodian's regulatory status, capital
   adequacy, and segregation practices before appointment, and annually
   thereafter;
2. Ensure a written sub-custody agreement confirms that Fund assets remain
   identifiable and protected from the sub-custodian's insolvency;
3. Remain liable to the Fund for the loss of any financial instrument held
   in custody, including by a sub-custodian, unless the loss results from
   an external event beyond reasonable control.

## Article 5 — Asset Loss Notification

The Depositary must notify the Fund Manager and the MFCA within 24 hours of
becoming aware of any loss, misappropriation, or unreconciled discrepancy
affecting Fund assets exceeding 0.05% of the Fund's net asset value.
Restitution of lost financial instruments to the Fund must occur without
undue delay.

## Article 6 — Depositary Replacement

A change of Depositary requires MFCA notification at least 30 calendar
days in advance, disclosure to investors, and confirmation that asset
transfer to the incoming Depositary will not disrupt dealing in the Fund's
units.
