# Best Execution and Order Handling for Fund Trading (REG-FM-115)

## Article 1 — Best Execution Obligation

A Fund Manager must take all sufficient steps to obtain the best possible
result for the Fund when executing orders, taking into account price,
cost, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size, and any other
relevant consideration.

## Article 2 — Execution Policy

The Fund Manager must maintain a written Order Execution Policy,
disclosed in summary to investors, identifying the venues and
counterparties used for each asset class and the relative importance
assigned to each execution factor in Article 1.

## Article 3 — Order Aggregation and Allocation

Where orders for multiple Funds are aggregated, the Fund Manager must
ensure aggregation does not work to the disadvantage of any Fund, and
must allocate the resulting trades in accordance with the pre-defined
methodology required under Article 3 of REG-FM-111.

## Article 4 — Monitoring and Review

The Fund Manager must monitor the effectiveness of its execution
arrangements at least quarterly, comparing execution quality across
venues and counterparties, and must review the Execution Policy at least
annually or upon a material change in market structure.

## Article 5 — Conflicts in Counterparty Selection

Selection of an affiliated broker or counterparty is permitted only where
it can be demonstrated that execution quality is at least equivalent to
that available from independent counterparties, and the arrangement is
disclosed under the related-party transaction requirements of Article 2
of REG-FM-111.

## Article 6 — Record Keeping

Records demonstrating compliance with the best execution obligation,
including transaction cost analysis, must be retained for 5 years and made
available to the MFCA within 10 business days of a request.

## Article 7 — Client Reporting

Upon reasonable request from an institutional investor, the Fund Manager
must demonstrate that orders relating to that investor's Fund holdings
were executed in accordance with the Execution Policy.
