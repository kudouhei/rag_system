# Sustainability (ESG) Disclosure Requirements (REG-FM-108)

## Article 1 — Fund Classification

Every Fund must be classified into one of three sustainability categories,
disclosed prominently in the Prospectus and KIID:

1. **Category A (Sustainable Objective)** — the Fund has sustainable
   investment as its primary objective and commits to a minimum percentage
   of sustainable investments;
2. **Category B (ESG Characteristics)** — the Fund promotes environmental
   or social characteristics as one of several factors, without a binding
   sustainable-investment minimum;
3. **Category C (No Sustainability Claim)** — the Fund does not promote
   ESG characteristics and may not use sustainability-related terms in its
   name or primary marketing.

## Article 2 — Pre-Contractual Disclosure

A Category A or Category B Fund must disclose, prior to subscription:

1. The environmental or social characteristics promoted, or the
   sustainable investment objective pursued;
2. The binding elements of the investment strategy used to attain those
   characteristics or objective;
3. The minimum proportion of investments used to meet the promoted
   characteristics or objective, expressed as a percentage of net assets;
4. The methodology and data sources used to measure attainment.

## Article 3 — Periodic Reporting

Category A and Category B Funds must publish an annual Sustainability
Report, within 4 months of the Fund's financial year end, disclosing the
actual proportion of sustainable investments achieved against the
committed minimum, and an explanation of any shortfall.

## Article 4 — Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) Statement

A Fund Manager managing more than MER 500,000,000 in aggregate assets must
publish an annual Principal Adverse Impact statement covering, at minimum:
greenhouse gas emissions intensity, board gender diversity of investee
companies, and exposure to controversial weapons, across its full product
range.

## Article 5 — Prohibition on Unsubstantiated Claims

A Fund may not be named or marketed using terms such as "sustainable,"
"green," "climate," or "impact" unless it is classified as Category A or
Category B and can substantiate the claim with the disclosures required
under Article 2. This provision must be read together with Article 5 of
REG-FM-107 on marketing communications.

## Article 6 — Reclassification and Notification

Where a Fund Manager reclassifies a Fund between categories, it must
notify the MFCA at least 20 business days in advance and provide investors
written notice of the change, including the reasons and any impact on
investment strategy.

## Article 7 — Supervisory Review

The MFCA conducts thematic reviews of ESG disclosure compliance at least
annually. Enforcement action for greenwashing may include reclassification
of the Fund to Category C, public censure, and administrative fines of up
to 3% of the Fund's net asset value.
