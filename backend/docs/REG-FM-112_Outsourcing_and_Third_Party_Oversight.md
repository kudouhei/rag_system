# Outsourcing and Third-Party Service Provider Oversight (REG-FM-112)

## Article 1 — Scope

This Regulation applies to any arrangement under which a Fund Manager
delegates a function that would otherwise be performed in-house, including
portfolio management, fund administration, transfer agency, and
technology/data hosting services, to a third-party service provider.

## Article 2 — Critical vs. Non-Critical Outsourcing

A Fund Manager must classify each outsourcing arrangement as "critical" or
"non-critical" based on the impact of a service failure on the Fund's
ability to operate, meet redemption obligations, or comply with applicable
regulation. Critical outsourcing arrangements are subject to enhanced due
diligence and notification requirements under Articles 3 and 5.

## Article 3 — Pre-Appointment Due Diligence

Before appointing a critical service provider, a Fund Manager must assess:

1. The provider's financial stability, operational capacity, and
   regulatory standing;
2. Data security and business continuity arrangements, including
   compliance with REG-FM-113;
3. Any sub-outsourcing by the provider, which must be disclosed and
   separately assessed;
4. Concentration risk where multiple critical functions are outsourced to
   the same provider or group.

## Article 4 — Outsourcing Agreement Requirements

A written outsourcing agreement must specify, at minimum: service levels
and performance metrics, audit and inspection rights for the Fund Manager
and the MFCA, data protection obligations, termination rights, and an exit
plan enabling transition to an alternative provider without material
disruption to Fund operations.

## Article 5 — Notification to the MFCA

A Fund Manager must notify the MFCA at least 30 calendar days before
entering into, materially amending, or terminating a critical outsourcing
arrangement. Notification must include the exit plan required under
Article 4 and confirmation that the arrangement does not impair the
Fund Manager's ability to meet its regulatory obligations.

## Article 6 — Ongoing Monitoring

The Fund Manager retains full responsibility for outsourced functions and
must monitor service provider performance at least quarterly, including
review of service-level breaches, incident reports, and independent audit
findings (e.g. SOC 2 or equivalent reports).

## Article 7 — Incident Reporting

Any material service disruption at a critical service provider affecting
NAV publication, dealing, or custody must be reported to the MFCA within
24 hours, together with an assessment of investor impact and remediation
timeline.
