# Fund Prospectus and Disclosure Requirements (REG-FM-101)

## Article 1 — Purpose and Scope

This Regulation sets out the minimum disclosure standards that a Fund Manager
must satisfy before offering units of a collective investment scheme
("Fund") to retail or institutional investors within the Republic of
Meridia. It applies to open-ended and closed-ended Funds, including UCITS-
equivalent schemes and exchange-traded funds (ETFs) registered with the
Meridian Financial Conduct Authority (MFCA).

## Article 2 — Mandatory Prospectus Contents

A Fund Manager shall not market or distribute units of a Fund unless the
Prospectus contains, at a minimum:

1. The Fund's investment objective, policy, and benchmark (if any);
2. A description of the risk profile, including a Synthetic Risk Indicator
   (SRI) on a 1–7 scale calculated in accordance with Annex B;
3. The total expense ratio (TER), management fee, performance fee formula
   (if applicable), and any soft-commission arrangements;
4. Historical performance for the preceding 5 years, or since inception if
   shorter, net of fees;
5. Identity and regulatory status of the Fund Manager, Depositary, and
   Auditor;
6. Redemption terms, including notice periods, dealing frequency, and any
   redemption gates permitted under REG-FM-106.

## Article 3 — Key Investor Information Document (KIID)

Every retail Fund must produce a Key Investor Information Document not
exceeding two A4 pages, updated at least annually and within 35 business
days of any material change to the Fund's risk profile or cost structure.
The KIID must be provided to a prospective investor free of charge prior to
subscription.

## Article 4 — Update Obligations

A Fund Manager must update the Prospectus without undue delay, and in any
case within 20 business days, following:

1. A change in investment strategy or risk profile;
2. A change of Depositary, Auditor, or key portfolio manager;
3. The introduction or removal of a performance fee;
4. Any regulatory action described in an Enforcement Notice concerning the
   Fund Manager.

## Article 5 — Language and Accessibility

Disclosure documents distributed to retail investors within the Republic of
Meridia must be made available in the official language(s) of the
jurisdiction of distribution. Disclosures may not be provided exclusively
in a language other than that used in the marketing communication.

## Article 6 — Record Keeping

The Fund Manager must retain all versions of the Prospectus and KIID, and
evidence of distribution, for a minimum of 7 years from the date the
document ceased to be current, and must produce these records to the MFCA
within 10 business days of a written request.

## Article 7 — Sanctions for Non-Compliance

Failure to comply with Articles 2–5 may result in an administrative fine of
up to 2% of the Fund's net asset value, suspension of marketing
authorisation, or referral for enforcement action under the MFCA
Enforcement Framework.
