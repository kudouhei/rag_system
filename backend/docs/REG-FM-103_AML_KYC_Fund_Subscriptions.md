# AML/KYC for Fund Subscriptions and Redemptions (REG-FM-103)

## Article 1 — Customer Due Diligence (CDD)

Before accepting a subscription, a Fund Manager or its appointed Transfer
Agent must complete Customer Due Diligence, including:

1. Verification of the investor's identity using a government-issued
   identification document or equivalent digital identity credential;
2. Verification of beneficial ownership for any investor that is a legal
   entity, trust, or nominee, down to the natural person(s) holding, directly
   or indirectly, more than 25% of capital or voting rights;
3. Screening against applicable sanctions lists and politically exposed
   persons (PEP) registers prior to the first subscription being accepted.

## Article 2 — Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)

Enhanced Due Diligence is required where an investor:

1. Is a Politically Exposed Person or a close associate/family member of one;
2. Is domiciled in, or funds are sourced from, a jurisdiction on the MFCA's
   published list of higher-risk jurisdictions;
3. Subscribes through a complex or opaque ownership structure without clear
   commercial rationale.

EDD measures must include obtaining senior management approval before
onboarding, establishing the source of wealth and source of funds, and
conducting enhanced ongoing monitoring at least every 6 months.

## Article 3 — Ongoing Monitoring

A Fund Manager must monitor investor activity throughout the business
relationship, with particular attention to:

1. Redemption requests inconsistent with the investor's stated investment
   horizon;
2. Subscriptions or redemptions structured to fall just below reporting
   thresholds;
3. Third-party payments not originating from an account in the investor's
   own name.

## Article 4 — Suspicious Activity Reporting

Where an employee of the Fund Manager forms a suspicion that a transaction
may be related to money laundering or terrorist financing, a Suspicious
Activity Report (SAR) must be filed with the Meridia Financial Intelligence
Unit within 3 business days. Tipping-off the investor concerned is
strictly prohibited and constitutes a criminal offence under the Meridia
AML Act.

## Article 5 — Reliance on Third Parties

A Fund Manager may rely on CDD performed by a regulated intermediary
(distributor or platform) only where a written reliance agreement is in
place and the Fund Manager retains ultimate responsibility for compliance
with this Regulation. Reliance does not extend to EDD measures under
Article 2, which must be performed or directly verified by the Fund
Manager.

## Article 6 — Record Retention

CDD records, transaction records, and SARs must be retained for a minimum
of 7 years following the end of the business relationship, and must be
produced to the MFCA or Meridia Financial Intelligence Unit within 5
business days of a request.

## Article 7 — Penalties

Breach of Articles 1, 2, or 4 may result in an administrative fine of up
to 5% of annual turnover of the Fund Manager, personal liability for the
designated Money Laundering Reporting Officer, and mandatory remediation
under MFCA supervision.
