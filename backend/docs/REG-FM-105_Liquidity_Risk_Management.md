# Liquidity Risk Management for Open-Ended Funds (REG-FM-105)

## Article 1 — Liquidity Risk Framework

Every Fund Manager operating an open-ended Fund must establish, document,
and maintain a Liquidity Risk Management Framework, approved by the Fund's
governing body, covering:

1. The liquidity profile of Fund assets relative to redemption terms;
2. Stress testing methodology and frequency (see Article 3);
3. The liquidity management tools available for use, and the governance
   process for activating them.

## Article 2 — Liquidity/Redemption Alignment

The Fund Manager must ensure the Fund's redemption terms (dealing
frequency, notice period, settlement period) are appropriate given the
liquidity of the underlying portfolio. A Fund investing more than 20% of
net assets in instruments that cannot be liquidated within 5 business days
under normal market conditions may not offer daily redemption without
additional liquidity management tools in place.

## Article 3 — Liquidity Stress Testing

Fund Managers must conduct liquidity stress tests at least quarterly, and
more frequently for Funds with elevated redemption risk, simulating:

1. A redemption shock of at least 20% of net assets within a single
   dealing day;
2. A simultaneous stress on the liquidity of underlying assets (e.g. a
   market-wide widening of bid-ask spreads);
3. Combined investor-side and asset-side stress scenarios as described in
   the MFCA Stress Testing Circular (CIRC-2024-03).

Stress test results must be reported to the Fund's governing body and
retained for at least 5 years.

## Article 4 — Liquidity Management Tools

The following tools may be used, where enabled in the Prospectus, to
manage liquidity mismatches:

1. **Redemption gates** (see REG-FM-106);
2. **Swing pricing** — adjusting the NAV to pass the cost of liquidity to
   transacting investors;
3. **Anti-dilution levies** — a fee charged on net subscriptions or
   redemptions;
4. **Redemption in kind** — for institutional investors only, subject to
   investor consent.

## Article 5 — Reporting to the MFCA

A Fund Manager must submit a Liquidity Risk Report to the MFCA within 15
business days following each quarterly stress test, and must notify the
MFCA within 24 hours if a stress test reveals that the Fund could not meet
redemption requests representing more than 30% of net assets within the
disclosed settlement period.

## Article 6 — Amendment Notice

This Regulation is supplemented by REG-FM-105A (2025), which introduces
additional mandatory liquidity management tools for money market funds.
Where REG-FM-105A conflicts with this Article, REG-FM-105A prevails for
money market funds only.
