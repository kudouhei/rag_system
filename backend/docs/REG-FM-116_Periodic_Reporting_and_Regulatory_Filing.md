# Periodic Reporting and Regulatory Filing Obligations (REG-FM-116)

## Article 1 — Annual and Semi-Annual Reports

A Fund Manager must publish an Annual Report within 4 months of the
Fund's financial year end, and a Semi-Annual Report within 2 months of the
Fund's half-year end, each including audited (annual) or reviewed
(semi-annual) financial statements, a portfolio statement, and a
comparison of actual performance against the benchmark disclosed in the
Prospectus.

## Article 2 — Quarterly Regulatory Return

Every Fund Manager must submit a Quarterly Regulatory Return to the MFCA
within 20 business days of quarter-end, covering: assets under management,
NAV error log summary (Article 3 of REG-FM-102), liquidity stress test
outcomes (Article 5 of REG-FM-105), and complaints statistics (Article 4
of REG-FM-114).

## Article 3 — Material Event Notification

The following events must be notified to the MFCA within the stated
timeframe, independent of routine periodic reporting:

| Event                                              | Notification Deadline |
|-----------------------------------------------------|------------------------|
| NAV publication suspension                          | 24 hours               |
| Redemption gate or full suspension of dealing        | 24 hours               |
| Material outsourcing incident (REG-FM-112)          | 24 hours               |
| Change of Depositary, Auditor, or key portfolio manager | 10 business days   |
| Breach of liquid asset thresholds (REG-FM-105A)     | 1 business day         |

## Article 4 — Regulatory Filing Accuracy

All filings submitted to the MFCA must be accurate and complete. A Fund
Manager identifying an error in a previously submitted filing must submit
a corrected filing within 5 business days of discovery, together with an
explanation of the error and its cause.

## Article 5 — Public Register

The MFCA maintains a public register of authorised Funds and Fund
Managers, including current regulatory status, any active enforcement
action, and historical Enforcement Notices. Fund Managers must notify the
MFCA of any change to information appearing on the public register within
10 business days.

## Article 6 — Late or Inaccurate Filing

Late submission of any report or return required under this Regulation,
or the submission of materially inaccurate information, is a reportable
compliance breach and may result in an administrative fine and increased
supervisory scrutiny, including more frequent on-site inspections.
