# Cross-Border Fund Distribution and Passporting (REG-FM-109)

## Article 1 — Passporting Notification

A Fund Manager domiciled in the Republic of Meridia intending to market a
Fund in another member jurisdiction of the Cross-Border Fund Passporting
Board (CFPB) framework must submit a passporting notification to the
MFCA, including:

1. The Prospectus, KIID, and constitutional documents in the language(s)
   required by the host jurisdiction;
2. Confirmation of the marketing arrangements and distribution channel(s)
   to be used;
3. A fee as prescribed in the MFCA Fee Schedule.

The MFCA must transmit a complete notification to the host regulator
within 10 business days of receipt. Marketing may commence once the host
regulator confirms receipt, or after 20 business days if no objection is
raised.

## Article 2 — Host Jurisdiction Obligations

Once passported, a Fund Manager must comply with the host jurisdiction's
local marketing and conduct rules (including advertising standards
equivalent to REG-FM-107) in addition to home-jurisdiction prudential
regulation. The MFCA remains the lead prudential supervisor for
matters relating to NAV calculation, custody, and liquidity risk
management.

## Article 3 — Facilities for Investors

Where a Fund is marketed to retail investors in a host jurisdiction, the
Fund Manager must maintain local facilities enabling investors to:

1. Subscribe and redeem units;
2. Obtain information about the Fund in the local language;
3. Submit complaints, which must be forwarded to the Fund Manager's
   complaints function under REG-FM-114 within 2 business days.

## Article 4 — Withdrawal of Passport

A Fund Manager withdrawing a Fund from marketing in a host jurisdiction
must provide at least 30 calendar days' notice to affected investors and
notify the MFCA and host regulator no later than the date the withdrawal
takes effect. Existing investors in the host jurisdiction retain full
redemption rights notwithstanding withdrawal of the marketing passport.

## Article 5 — Third-Country Distribution

Distribution of a Meridia-domiciled Fund into a jurisdiction outside the
CFPB framework ("third country") is not covered by this passporting
regime and requires separate authorisation under the third country's local
law. The Fund Manager remains responsible for ensuring third-country
marketing materials comply with Article 1 of REG-FM-107 regardless of
where distribution occurs.

## Article 6 — Reporting

Fund Managers must submit an annual Cross-Border Distribution Report to
the MFCA, listing all jurisdictions in which each Fund is actively
marketed and the aggregate assets under management attributable to
investors in each jurisdiction.
