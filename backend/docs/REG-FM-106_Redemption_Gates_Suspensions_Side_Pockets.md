# Redemption Gates, Suspensions and Side Pockets (REG-FM-106)

## Article 1 — Redemption Gates

A Fund Manager may apply a redemption gate — limiting redemptions on a
given dealing day to a fixed percentage of the Fund's net assets — only
where:

1. The mechanism and the applicable percentage (minimum 5%, maximum 20% of
   net assets per dealing day) are disclosed in the Prospectus prior to
   investor subscription;
2. Gated redemption requests are treated pro-rata among all investors
   redeeming on that dealing day; and
3. Investors are notified of the gate's activation within 24 hours,
   including the expected duration and criteria for lifting it.

A gate may not remain in place for more than 3 consecutive dealing periods
without an updated notification to the MFCA and investors explaining the
rationale for continuation.

## Article 2 — Full Suspension of Dealing

A Fund Manager may suspend subscriptions and/or redemptions in
exceptional circumstances, including:

1. Closure of a market representing a material part of the Fund's assets;
2. A NAV suspension under Article 5 of REG-FM-102;
3. Circumstances where continuing to deal would be materially prejudicial
   to the interests of remaining investors.

Suspension must be approved by the Fund's governing body (or, in urgent
cases, ratified within 24 hours), reported to the MFCA within 24 hours, and
reviewed at least every 20 business days for as long as it remains in
effect.

## Article 3 — Side Pockets

Where a portion of the Fund's portfolio becomes illiquid or difficult to
value reliably (e.g. due to a market dislocation or an issuer default), the
Fund Manager may, with Depositary sign-off, segregate the affected assets
into a side pocket, subject to:

1. Existing investors at the time of segregation participate pro-rata in
   the side pocket; new subscribers after segregation do not;
2. The side pocket is valued and reported separately from the main Fund's
   NAV, using the valuation hierarchy in Article 2 of REG-FM-102;
3. Investors are given written notice, including the estimated realisation
   timeline, within 5 business days of segregation.

## Article 4 — Investor Communication Standard

All communications relating to gates, suspensions, or side pockets must be
clear, fair, and not misleading, and must avoid language that
understates the risk of delayed access to capital. Template wording is
provided in MFCA Guidance Circular CIRC-2023-11.

## Article 5 — Regulatory Escalation

Three or more gate or suspension events by the same Fund Manager within a
rolling 12-month period will trigger an MFCA supervisory review of the
Fund Manager's liquidity risk framework under REG-FM-105.
