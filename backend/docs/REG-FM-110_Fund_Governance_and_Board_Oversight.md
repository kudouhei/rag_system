# Fund Governance and Board Oversight Requirements (REG-FM-110)

## Article 1 — Governing Body Composition

Every Fund must have a governing body (board of directors or trustee
board) comprising at least 3 members, of whom at least one-third must be
independent of the Fund Manager, its parent group, and any material
service provider to the Fund.

## Article 2 — Fitness and Propriety

Each member of the governing body must satisfy the MFCA's fitness and
propriety criteria, including relevant experience in asset management,
absence of disqualifying regulatory findings, and completion of at least
15 hours of continuing professional development annually relevant to fund
governance.

## Article 3 — Core Governance Duties

The governing body must, at minimum:

1. Approve the Fund's Liquidity Risk Management Framework (REG-FM-105) and
   review it at least annually;
2. Approve and oversee the Valuation Policy referenced in REG-FM-102;
3. Review the performance of the Fund Manager, Depositary, and Auditor at
   least annually against the terms of their respective mandates;
4. Approve any material change to the investment strategy, fee structure,
   or sustainability classification before implementation;
5. Oversee the Fund Manager's conflicts of interest register maintained
   under REG-FM-111.

## Article 4 — Meeting Frequency and Minutes

The governing body must meet at least quarterly, with minutes recording
attendance, key decisions, and dissenting views. Minutes must be retained
for at least 7 years and made available to the MFCA upon request within 5
business days.

## Article 5 — Independent Director Duties

Independent members of the governing body have a specific duty to
challenge the Fund Manager on matters affecting investor interests,
including fee levels, valuation of illiquid assets, and the use of
liquidity management tools under REG-FM-105 and REG-FM-106. An independent
member may escalate unresolved concerns directly to the MFCA.

## Article 6 — Removal and Replacement

Removal of an independent governing body member requires a documented
rationale approved by a majority of the remaining governing body and
notification to the MFCA within 10 business days, together with the
reasons for removal.

## Article 7 — Annual Governance Self-Assessment

The governing body must complete an annual self-assessment of its
effectiveness, covering composition, independence, and the adequacy of
information provided by the Fund Manager, and submit a summary to the
MFCA together with the Fund's annual report.
