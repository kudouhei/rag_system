# Enforcement Notice ENF-2024-07 — Late NAV Disclosure and Investor Notification Failure

## 1. Summary of Findings

Following a supervisory review, the MFCA found that a Meridia-authorised
Fund Manager ("the Firm," anonymised in this public notice) failed to
publish the Net Asset Value (NAV) of one of its open-ended equity funds
within the timeframe disclosed in the Fund's Prospectus on four occasions
between January and June 2024, in breach of Article 1 of REG-FM-102.

## 2. Nature of the Breach

Investigation established that:

1. A change to the Firm's third-party fund administration platform,
   implemented without adequate pre-migration testing, caused recurring
   NAV calculation delays of between 6 and 14 hours beyond the disclosed
   publication deadline;
2. The Firm did not report the first two occurrences to the MFCA within
   24 hours as required, in breach of Article 6 of REG-FM-102 and Article
   3 of REG-FM-116 (Material Event Notification);
3. Affected investors placing redemption orders during the delay window
   were not informed of the NAV publication delay at the time they
   submitted their orders, contrary to the fair treatment expectations
   underlying REG-FM-102 and REG-FM-114.

## 3. Root Cause

The Firm's outsourcing due diligence prior to the platform migration did
not adequately test the new administrator's NAV calculation process under
peak volume conditions, a deficiency inconsistent with the pre-appointment
due diligence standard in Article 3 of REG-FM-112.

## 4. Remedial Action Taken by the Firm

Following MFCA intervention, the Firm:

1. Reverted to enhanced manual NAV verification procedures pending
   resolution of the platform issue;
2. Compensated all investors who transacted during the four delay periods
   in accordance with the materiality-based compensation approach in
   Article 3 of REG-FM-102;
3. Commissioned an independent third-party review of its outsourcing
   governance framework, completed within 90 days of the MFCA's initial
   inquiry.

## 5. Sanction

The MFCA imposed an administrative fine of MER 340,000 on the Firm for
breaches of Article 1 and Article 6 of REG-FM-102 and Article 3 of
REG-FM-116, reflecting: the recurring nature of the breach (four
instances), the Firm's delayed self-reporting, and mitigating credit for
full cooperation and voluntary investor compensation. The Firm was also
required to submit enhanced quarterly outsourcing oversight reports to the
MFCA for a period of 12 months.

## 6. Supervisory Guidance for the Industry

Fund Managers undertaking a change of fund administrator or other critical
outsourced NAV-calculation function should, in light of this case:

1. Conduct parallel-run testing of the incoming platform against the
   outgoing platform for at least one full quarterly cycle before cutover;
2. Ensure investor-facing dealing desks have a documented escalation
   protocol to flag potential NAV delays to investors placing orders in
   real time;
3. Review their Material Event Notification procedures under Article 3 of
   REG-FM-116 to ensure the 24-hour reporting deadline is embedded in
   operational runbooks, not only compliance policy documents.
